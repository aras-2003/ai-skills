# Executive Role Evaluation Model

## Evidence classes
- **FACT** — explicitly stated in a reliable source.
- **STRONG INFERENCE** — strongly supported but not explicit.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible and requires validation.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence.

## Role archetypes
Enterprise CIO; Product/Engineering CTO; Transformation Executive; Deputy CIO/Technology COO; Technology Strategy/Chief of Staff; Domain Technology Leader; IT Operations Leader; Advisory/Governance Leader; Inflated-title role.

If uncertain, show the two most plausible archetypes.

## Qualitative assessment contract

### Role Quality
Assess these dimensions individually:
- mandate & decision authority;
- organizational ownership;
- technology scope;
- strategic influence;
- budget/investment authority;
- transformation leverage;
- business accountability;
- executive positioning.

Overall label:
- **STRONG** — evidence supports broad authority/scope with no critical structural contradiction.
- **MIXED** — meaningful strengths plus material limitations/unknowns.
- **WEAK** — evidenced structural scope/authority limitations dominate.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence for a defensible overall view.

Do not average UNKNOWN dimensions and do not convert labels to a numeric total.

### Candidate Fit
Use **STRONG / GOOD / PARTIAL / GAP / UNKNOWN** for: scope experience, leadership scale, transformation experience, technology breadth, executive stakeholder management, industry relevance, international exposure, budget ownership, delivery credibility and role-specific technical credibility.

### Career Value
Use **HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN** across scope expansion, organizational scale, executive exposure, budget ownership, global complexity, technology breadth, market signalling and learning density.

The overall career-value label is a reasoned synthesis, not arithmetic.

## Critical gates
Treat as critical/potentially critical when material:
- no meaningful decision authority;
- responsibility without control of people/budget/priorities;
- role materially narrower than title;
- no team where organizational leadership is core to the goal;
- promised future mandate without current sponsor/governance;
- transformation accountability without escalation/decision rights;
- lifestyle/travel conflict with a hard constraint.

A critical gate can force `INVESTIGATE` when evidence is incomplete; it does not automatically imply `SKIP`.

## Decision states
- **PURSUE** — evidence supports active investment.
- **INVESTIGATE** — potentially attractive; critical unknowns can change the decision.
- **LOW PRIORITY** — some value, but material limitations reduce attention.
- **SKIP** — structural mismatch/downside is sufficiently evidenced.

Never mechanically map an assessment label to a decision.

## Confidence
- **HIGH** — key structural facts are well supported.
- **MEDIUM** — important uncertainty remains.
- **LOW** — critical structural facts are missing/conflicting.

Confidence reflects evidence completeness, not conviction.

## Numeric requests
There is no calibrated /100 aggregation in this repository. If a user explicitly requests a numeric score, do not invent a pseudo-precise total. State this limitation and provide the same qualitative dimensions plus evidence/confidence unless a separate, explicit scoring contract has been supplied for that task.
