# Operating Model Review

## Purpose
Assess whether the organisation's division of responsibility and coordination model supports its strategic goals.

## Procedure
1. Identify major value streams/functions/business units and their mandates.
2. Map global/central/local ownership and decision boundaries.
3. Review interfaces where work or decisions cross units.
4. Identify duplication, gaps, excessive handoffs, unclear accountability and over-centralisation/fragmentation.
5. Test whether governance and funding mechanisms match the intended model.
6. Recommend design principles and priority operating-model changes.

## Decision rules
- Structure alone is not the operating model.
- Centralise where scale/consistency matters; localise where context/speed matters.
- Accountability must match authority and resources.

## Output contract
Current model summary; key interfaces; top misfits; design principles; priority changes; unresolved trade-offs.

## Model guidance
Default: **standard**. Escalate for major multi-country redesign or highly contested autonomy decisions.

## Quality checks
- [ ] Global/local split explicit.
- [ ] Interfaces reviewed.
- [ ] Authority/resource alignment checked.
