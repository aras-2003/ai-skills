# Commerce Product Deep Dive Workflow

## Purpose
Evaluate one product opportunity end-to-end before committing to a market test.

## Preconditions
Require a concrete product or product concept, target market and intended sales channel. If the product is still only a broad category idea, route to `product-opportunity-discovery` first.

## Stages
1. **Frame the thesis** — target customer, problem, product, target market, source-market reference if any, intended channel and price hypothesis.
2. **Validate the problem** — use `problem-demand-validation`.
3. **Check market transfer** — use `market-transferability-review` when the thesis relies on success in another country.
4. **Map competition** — use `competition-landscape-review`; separate product, brand, distribution and acquisition competition.
5. **Test differentiation** — use `differentiation-review`, especially when a premium price must beat cheap generics.
6. **Review economics** — use `unit-economics-review`; fail early when CAC headroom or sensitivity is structurally weak.
7. **Review sourcing** — use `supplier-viability-review`.
8. **Review regulation** — use `commerce-regulatory-risk-review` only to the depth needed for commercial screening; route to professional verification where required.
9. **Review acquisition fit** — use `acquisition-fit-review` with the intended channel and allowable CAC.
10. **Synthesize gates** — problem, product/differentiation, economics, acquisition, supply/regulation.
11. **Choose next action** — TEST NOW / RESEARCH ONE BLOCKER / DEFER / KILL.
12. **Define smallest useful test** when the opportunity passes: offer, landing page, creatives, spend cap, success/failure evidence and stop conditions.

## Decision rules
- Trend popularity does not override weak economics.
- High margin does not override regulation or sourcing risk.
- A premium price must have an observable reason to buy.
- Do not recommend a market test when a fatal gate is already evidenced.
- Do not extend desk research when a cheap real-world test would resolve the main uncertainty.
- Keep Evidence / Hypothesis / Unknown distinct.

### Smallest next test
Only when justified: hypothesis, setup, spend/stock cap, metrics, success threshold, stop condition.

## Model guidance
Default: standard. Strong is escalation-only for high-capital, highly regulated or strategically coupled decisions.

## Stop conditions
Stop and KILL when a fatal economics, regulatory, logistics or differentiation constraint is evidenced and not plausibly fixable.
Stop research and move to testing when the major remaining uncertainty is empirical conversion/CAC rather than desk-research evidence.
