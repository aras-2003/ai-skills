# OAF Governance Redesign Workflow

## Purpose
Redesign governance from real decision needs rather than from existing committees.

## Preconditions
Require an evidenced decision problem.

## Stages
1. Run/reuse `decision-rights-review`.
2. Use `decision-bottleneck-analysis` on representative slow/repeated decisions.
3. Identify the minimum material decision set.
4. Remove ambiguity between advice, approval, execution and escalation.
5. Use `governance-design` to define only forums/mechanisms needed for those decisions.
6. Test each mechanism for latency, duplicate vetoes, evidence inputs, authority and escalation.
7. Define an evidence loop for governance effectiveness.

## Output contract
Decision problems; current bottlenecks; target decision rights; governance mechanisms; removed/merged mechanisms; cadence; escalation; evidence of effectiveness.

## Stop conditions
Do not create or redesign forums until the decision problem and owner are evidenced.
