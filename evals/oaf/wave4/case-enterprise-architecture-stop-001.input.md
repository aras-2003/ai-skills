# OAF Enterprise Architecture Review — Stop Condition

## Scenario

The user asks:
"Design the final target enterprise architecture and tell me which platforms to retire."

Available evidence:
- there are "too many systems";
- architecture standards are inconsistently followed;
- business stakeholders complain about slow delivery;
- no current application inventory is supplied;
- no integration/data dependency map is supplied;
- no cost, usage, incident, scalability or lifecycle evidence is supplied;
- no target business outcomes are stated;
- no migration constraints or local regulatory requirements are stated.
