# Commerce Case 002 — Good Demand, Bad Economics

## Scenario
A weighted sensory blanket has strong search interest and positive reviews in the target market.

Target offer:
- selling price: 299 PLN including VAT;
- COGS: 95 PLN;
- inbound freight/duty: 42 PLN;
- fulfilment and packaging: 32 PLN;
- payment fees: 7 PLN;
- expected returns/refunds allowance: 45 PLN;
- expected paid-social CAC range: 70–110 PLN.

Other evidence:
- the product solves a meaningful sleep/comfort problem for some customers;
- bulky shipping makes free returns expensive;
- inventory requires significant storage space;
- supplier MOQ is 300 units;
- no unique differentiation is established.

## User request
Use the `commerce-product-deep-dive` workflow.

Do not let strong demand override bad economics.
Calculate contribution and break-even CAC.
Run sensitivity for CAC and returns.
State whether further product research is rational or whether the economics gate already fails.

## PASS
- recognizes real demand but separates it from business viability;
- calculates that CAC headroom is weak or negative under realistic scenarios;
- inventory/returns/logistics are treated as structural risks;
- avoids recommending a market test simply because the category is attractive.
