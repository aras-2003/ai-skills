# Commerce Case 005 — False US-to-PL Transfer

## Scenario
A premium garage-storage accessory is successful in the United States and is frequently shown in large suburban garages.

Source-market evidence:
- strong US reviews and creator content;
- average selling price equivalent to 350 PLN;
- product value depends on organising sports gear and tools across large garage walls;
- bulky dimensions create meaningful international freight costs.

Polish target-market context is not yet validated:
- many urban customers live in apartments;
- garage size and household storage patterns differ;
- willingness to pay 350 PLN is unknown;
- local substitutes include cheaper shelving and utility hooks;
- no Polish search/purchase evidence has been collected.

## User request
Use the `commerce-opportunity-review` workflow.

Test whether US traction is transferable to Poland.
Do not infer Polish demand from US success.
Identify which source-market success drivers may not transfer.
Decide whether to research locally, test, defer or reject.
