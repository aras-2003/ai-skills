# Transformation Blueprint — Case 001

## Scenario

Leadership has selected a federated shared-capability operating-model direction after a validated OAF diagnosis and pilot decision.

Agreed target principles:
- enterprise standards for security, data and interoperability;
- shared platform capabilities with funded enterprise ownership;
- local markets retain business prioritisation and bounded local configuration;
- platform exceptions are evidence-based, time-bounded and owned;
- shared capabilities use pooled or explicit enterprise funding;
- governance should be minimal and decision-led.

Known constraints:
- current global platform lead times are too long;
- funding is still mostly local;
- two markets already depend on duplicated integration tooling;
- one global data team is capacity constrained;
- no enterprise capability owner has yet been formally appointed;
- leadership wants a 90-day mobilisation plan before approving a multi-year transformation.
