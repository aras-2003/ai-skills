# Acquisition Fit Review

## Purpose
Test whether the product can be explained and sold efficiently through the intended acquisition channel.

## Preconditions
Require a target audience, intended channel and at least provisional offer. CAC economics may be unresolved, but then profitability must remain unresolved too.

## Inputs and evidence
Use observable product/demo properties, actual creative/channel evidence where available, policy constraints and explicit break-even/target CAC from unit economics. Virality, CTR or engagement alone are not profitability evidence.

## Uncertainty and tool failure
If channel data, current platform policy or CAC headroom cannot be checked, label the relevant claim unverified. Do not invent CAC, conversion rate, spend cap or benchmark.

## Procedure
1. State primary channel and target audience.
2. Identify visible problem, demo, before/after, emotional hook and proof opportunities.
3. Generate several distinct creative angles, not variants of one message.
4. Compare likely acquisition difficulty with explicit CAC headroom when available.
5. Flag policy/platform restrictions and weak demonstrability.
6. Distinguish product appeal from channel fit and profitable acquisition.

## Decision rules
- Great product + weak creative potential can be weak DTC.
- Viral content != profitable acquisition.
- One strong creative angle is fragile.
- Acquisition fit must be judged against CAC headroom, not engagement alone.
- If CAC headroom is unknown, acquisition economics remain provisional.
- Do not invent target CAC or media budget.

## Output contract
Angle | hook | proof/demo | audience | likely friction | evidence/unknown.
Then: acquisition fit = strong / testable / weak / unresolved.

## Quality checks
- [ ] Channel/audience are explicit.
- [ ] Virality is not treated as conversion proof.
- [ ] CAC headroom is sourced or marked unknown.
- [ ] No invented spend cap or target CAC.
