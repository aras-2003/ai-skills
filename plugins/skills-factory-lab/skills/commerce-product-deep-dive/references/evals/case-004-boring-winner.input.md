# Commerce Case 004 — Boring Winner

## Scenario
A simple reusable crevice-cleaning brush for kitchens, bathrooms and window tracks has no obvious viral trend.

Evidence:
- visible dirt/problem is easy to demonstrate in video;
- generic alternatives exist but many reviews complain about weak bristles and poor grip;
- target bundle price: 69 PLN including VAT;
- COGS plus inbound freight: 11 PLN;
- fulfilment, packaging and fees: 14 PLN;
- returns allowance: 3 PLN;
- supplier MOQ: 200 bundles;
- multiple suppliers are available;
- product is small, light and non-fragile;
- no unusual regulatory burden is identified;
- at least four distinct creative/demo angles are plausible;
- no validated CAC exists.
- for this synthetic case, use an explicit VAT rate of 23%; listed COGS/freight, fulfilment/packaging/fees and return allowance are net economic variable costs per order; the 3 PLN return allowance is expected loss per order.

## User request
Use the `commerce-product-deep-dive` workflow.

Do not penalise the product because it is unglamorous or not trending.
Evaluate demand/problem, differentiation, economics, sourcing and acquisition fit.
State whether the remaining uncertainty is best resolved by more research or a small paid market test.
