# Transformation Blueprint — Case 001

## Scenario

Leadership has selected a federated shared-capability operating-model direction after a validated OAF diagnosis and pilot decision.

Agreed target principles:
- enterprise standards for security, data and interoperability;
- shared platform capabilities with funded enterprise ownership;
- local markets retain business prioritisation and bounded local configuration;
- platform exceptions are evidence-based, time-bounded and owned;
- shared capabilities use pooled or explicit enterprise funding;
- governance should be minimal and decision-led.

Known constraints:
- current global platform lead times are too long;
- funding is still mostly local;
- two markets already depend on duplicated integration tooling;
- one global data team is capacity constrained;
- no enterprise capability owner has yet been formally appointed;
- leadership wants a 90-day mobilisation plan before approving a multi-year transformation.

## Test objective

Evaluate whether `transformation-blueprint`:
1. traces workstreams to diagnosed problems and target principles;
2. separates 30–90 day mobilisation from longer-term rollout;
3. identifies capacity/dependency constraints;
4. uses owner-to-confirm where ownership is unresolved;
5. includes pilots and stage gates;
6. avoids inventing a detailed multi-year roadmap unsupported by evidence;
7. distinguishes outcomes from activity.

## Expected behaviors

PASS if:
- workstreams cover funding, platform service/lead time, exception decision rights, shared capability ownership, integration/data dependencies and evidence loops;
- the constrained data team appears as a sequencing/capacity issue;
- unresolved enterprise capability ownership remains unresolved;
- stage gates can continue/adjust/stop;
- 90-day mobilisation is concrete but bounded;
- longer-term sequencing remains conditional.

FAIL if:
- it assigns named owners without evidence;
- turns every issue into a project;
- creates a detailed multi-year roadmap despite the stated decision boundary;
- treats meetings/deliverables as transformation outcomes.
