# OAF Transformation Design Workflow

## Purpose
Convert an evidenced organisational diagnosis and selected target direction into a bounded, adaptive transformation plan without pretending unresolved design choices are settled.

## Preconditions
Require:
- validated diagnosis;
- selected or explicitly bounded target direction;
- critical decision rights/operating-model choices sufficiently resolved;
- known major constraints.

## Stages
1. **Reuse diagnosis and target direction** — do not recreate strategy or operating-model analysis unless evidence changed.
2. **Confirm transformation boundary** — what must change now, what is deferred, and what remains hypothesis.
3. **Identify structural change themes** — operating model, decision rights, capabilities, architecture, portfolio/funding, evidence loops.
4. **Use `transformation-blueprint`** — translate only evidenced changes into workstreams.
5. **Sequence by dependency and capacity** — foundations, pilots, scale, retirement.
6. **Define decision gates** — continue / adjust / stop criteria and who decides.
7. **Protect operations** — surface temporary-state complexity, service continuity and transition risk.
8. **Define 30–90 day mobilisation** — decisions, owners-to-confirm, pilots, evidence collection and enabling work.
9. **Define longer horizon cautiously** — only where dependencies and target direction are sufficiently stable.
10. **Close the evidence loop** — specify checkpoints that can change scope, sequence or assumptions.

## Decision rules
- A roadmap is not evidence of readiness.
- Do not schedule unresolved design choices as committed deliverables.
- Quick wins must reduce risk or advance the target state.
- Use pilots where reversibility is valuable.
- Capacity constraints can override desired timing.
- Transformation progress is measured by outcomes and decision quality, not activity volume.

## Output contract
- transformation boundary;
- diagnosis-to-change traceability;
- workstreams;
- sequence and critical path;
- 30–90 day mobilisation;
- stage gates;
- transition risks;
- outcome/evidence measures;
- unresolved decisions;
- longer-horizon direction where supported.

## Model guidance
Default: **standard** for bounded transformation design.
Escalate to strong for enterprise-wide multi-year sequencing, major transition economics, highly coupled global programs or contested target-state assumptions.

## Stop conditions
Stop if the target direction is not sufficiently selected.
Stop at mobilisation if evidence does not support a credible longer-term sequence.
