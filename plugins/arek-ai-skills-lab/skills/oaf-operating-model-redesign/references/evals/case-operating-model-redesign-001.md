# OAF Operating Model Redesign — Workflow Case 001

## Scenario

A multinational technology organisation has:
- strong local market teams with direct business accountability;
- global platform, architecture and cybersecurity functions;
- duplicated local solutions in CRM, integration and data tooling;
- global platforms that some markets bypass because delivery lead times are too long;
- unclear ownership for cross-market capabilities;
- investment budgets held locally, while enterprise standards are defined globally;
- multiple global/local escalation forums;
- leadership wants to reduce duplication but does not want to slow local innovation.

A recent diagnostic has already established:
- real global/local tension;
- unclear decision rights for platform exceptions;
- interface failures between global platform teams and local delivery teams;
- inconsistent funding authority for shared capabilities.

## Test objective

Evaluate whether `oaf-operating-model-redesign`:
1. reuses the diagnosis instead of restarting generic analysis;
2. uses global/local and interface specialist logic selectively;
3. defines design principles before options;
4. compares 2–3 bounded operating-model options;
5. tests authority/accountability/resources, interfaces, funding and transition risk;
6. avoids jumping directly to an org chart;
7. delays governance design until a target direction is chosen.

## Failure conditions

FAIL if it:
- recommends centralisation as the default;
- creates a new org chart without option trade-offs;
- treats global standards as automatically superior;
- ignores local speed/responsiveness;
- creates governance forums before decision rights are resolved.
